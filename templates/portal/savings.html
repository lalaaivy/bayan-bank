Savings Services

Physical cash deposits are processed at a Bayan Bank branch. Savings withdrawals can be requested online and transferred to your regular bank account after staff approval.

Deposit Money

Visit a branch and ask authorized staff to record your physical cash deposit.

Available savings terms:
3 Months 6 Months 12 Months
Every branch cash deposit becomes a separate locked term deposit. Interest is credited upon maturity.
Withdraw Money

Request any amount up to your total savings account balance. You do not need to select an individual deposit or wait for a deposit to mature.

Staff approval immediately transfers the approved amount from your savings account to your regular bank account. Both balances and the transaction ledger are updated.
