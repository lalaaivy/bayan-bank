Branch Services

Cash deposits and cash withdrawals are processed in person at a Bayan Bank branch by authorized staff.

Deposit Money

Visit a branch and deposit cash into your regular bank account. Once the deposit is recorded, you can transfer the available funds online into your savings account and select a savings term.

Early Withdrawal

Savings deposits cannot be withdrawn freely before maturity. Submit an early-withdrawal request through this page. An authorized staff member must review and approve the request. Early withdrawal forfeits interest.
