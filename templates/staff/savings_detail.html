Savings Withdrawal Requests

Clients can request a partial or full withdrawal against their total SavingsAccount balance. Approved requests are automatically transferred to the client's regular bank account.

Withdrawal process:
  1. The client submits an amount greater than ₱0 and no greater than the available savings account balance.
  2. Staff reviews and approves or rejects the request.
  3. Upon approval, the requested amount is immediately transferred to the client's regular bank account.
  4. The savings balance and regular account balance are updated together.
  5. The system records the corresponding transfer-out and transfer-in ledger entries.
